# v0048 — Encode the photographed INV001 stack

Date: 2026-08-24

Owner-supplied photographs support the established 85 mm HAT length, Raspberry
Pi mounting pattern, and a nominal 16 mm brass HAT spacer. Because the caliper's
digital display was off, the images are treated as fit evidence rather than
sub-millimeter metrology.

The CAD source now expresses the vertical envelope as a 16 mm nominal standoff,
1.6 mm HAT PCB, and 7.4 mm topside component/wiring/air allowance. The resulting
25 mm Pi-top-to-stack-top envelope and 8 mm gap below the base rim are unchanged,
so the printable 100.6 x 71.6 x 42.4 mm shell remains unchanged. The generated
validation report records the stack values and evidence limitation.

Base, lid, and power actuator were regenerated and revalidated as single-body,
watertight, winding-consistent positive-volume meshes. The installed Bambu Studio
X2D/PETG validation passed with the left/main nozzle, no supports, warnings, or
filament changes, and a 2 h 29 m 45 s estimate; temporary G-code was discarded.
Direct powered-down measurement, the 12 mm fit coupon, and thermal validation
remain required before the final PETG print. ADR-0036 records the conservative
evidence policy. This snapshot is immutable.
