## State captured

- Prime has a tracked generic `software-security-review` skill.
- Reviews begin with capability and trust-boundary discovery and activate only
  relevant modules rather than assuming a web framework, language, or runtime.
- Conditional coverage includes web/API, identity, memory and resource lifecycle,
  storage, subprocess/plugin/agent, network/IPC, cryptography, concurrency,
  supply chain, updates, deployment, and specialized domains.
- Confirmed findings require evidence-backed attack paths and confidence.
  Reliability, defense in depth, design assumptions, and unverified hypotheses
  are reported separately.
- Memory review distinguishes manual, managed, pooled, cross-language, GPU, and
  secret-buffer lifecycles.
- The prior report-only comparison identified two unresolved WebUI boundaries:
  spoofable loopback proxy identity headers and shared-user agent/console access.

`~/.prime/agent/skills/software-security-review` directory to return Prime's
