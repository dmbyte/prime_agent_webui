# v0047 — Compact equal-length Pi 5 and INV001 enclosure

Date: 2026-08-24

The owner confirmed that the relevant iUniker INV001 is the same 85 mm length as
Raspberry Pi 5. The parametric model now centers both boards within a 95 x 66 x
37.6 mm clear cavity and shortens the finished enclosure from 107.6 to 100.6 mm;
width and total height remain 71.6 and 42.4 mm. Lid towers moved closer to the
rounded outer corners to avoid the centered PCB outline and mounting standoffs.

The compact base, lid, and power actuator were regenerated and remain single-body,
watertight, winding-consistent, positive-volume meshes. Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61
successfully sliced all three together with the resolved X2D 0.4 mm, PETG Basic,
and 0.20 mm Standard profiles plus the recorded functional overrides. The result
uses the left/main nozzle, has zero filament changes, zero support features, no
warnings, and estimates 2 h 29 m 46 s. Temporary G-code was discarded. Physical
width, connector, button, stack-height, thermal, and fit-coupon validation remain
required. ADR-0035 records the corrected envelope decision. This snapshot is
immutable.
