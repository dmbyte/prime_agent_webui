# v0046 — X2D PETG Basic enclosure tuning

Date: 2026-08-24

The Raspberry Pi 5/iUniker INV001 enclosure is now tuned for Bambu PETG Basic on
a Bambu Lab X2D using its stock 0.4 mm left/main nozzle. Seven-nozzle-width 2.8 mm
walls, 0.35 mm-per-side lid clearance, and 2.7 mm M3 pilots replace the generic
values. Three rib-separated Ethernet/USB bays replace the original 54 mm combined
opening and reduce the longest wall bridge to less than 17 mm, allowing the base,
lid, and button to remain support-free without auxiliary-nozzle material.

An X2D-specific guide records Bambu Studio setup, orientations, strength settings,
fit-coupon strategy, and post-processing while deliberately inheriting nozzle,
bed, flow, retraction, and cooling values from Bambu's maintained PETG Basic
preset. The regenerated 107.6 x 71.6 x 42.4 mm design again exports three
single-body, watertight, winding-consistent, positive-volume STLs plus updated GLB
and PNG previews. Physical board, button, plug, slicer, and thermal validation
remain required. Bambu Studio 02.08.02.61 also sliced all three parts with the
resolved factory X2D/PETG profiles, four walls, 25% gyroid, five top/bottom layers,
left/main nozzle only, zero support features, zero filament changes, no warnings,
and a 2 h 37 m 42 s estimate. Temporary G-code was discarded and only the
settings/results report remains. ADR-0034 records the material/printer decision.
This snapshot is immutable.
