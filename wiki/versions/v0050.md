# v0050 — Adapt the supplied v14 controls and cooling

Date: 2026-08-24

The owner supplied four v14 STL parts as reference geometry for the Pi 5 native
button, adjacent status light, and enclosure ventilation. Mesh inspection found
the button's 10.6 x 8.3 x 5.6 mm overall envelope, its offset oval light window,
and its short-end placement. The reference shells use a honeycomb top, long floor
slots, and narrow wall slots.

The iUniker enclosure now uses a newly parameterized equivalent button with the
LED window through its face, a roughly 9 x 4 mm shared stem/light opening, long
parallel floor intakes, a honeycomb lid exhaust, and 12 mm vertical slots around
all four upper walls. The microSD access opening was removed at the owner's
request. The iUniker hardware stack, case size, connector bays, lid fasteners,
and PETG clearances remain specific to this design rather than copied wholesale.

All three printable parts regenerate as single-body, watertight,
winding-consistent positive-volume meshes. X2D/PETG slice validation passed on
the left/main nozzle with no supports, warnings, or filament changes and a
2 h 41 m 9 s estimate; temporary G-code was discarded. Physical button travel,
LED visibility, connector clearance, and thermal performance still require the
prescribed fit coupon and assembled test. ADR-0038 records the reference-adaptation
decision. This snapshot is immutable.
